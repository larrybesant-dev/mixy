MixVy Child Safety Standards

Effective October 6, 2026

MixVy is operated by Southern Supply Services and is intended for adults age 18 and older.

Zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE)

MixVy strictly prohibits child sexual abuse material (CSAM), child sexual exploitation, grooming, and any content or behavior that endangers minors. Accounts found to violate this standard are permanently removed, and associated content is preserved for law enforcement as required by law.

Age requirement

MixVy requires users to be at least 18 years old to create an account. Accounts suspected of belonging to minors or of being used to target minors are suspended and investigated.

In-app reporting

Users can report any account, profile, message, room, or piece of content directly within the app. Reports are reviewed by our moderation team and may result in content removal, account suspension, or permanent bans. Reports describing child sexual abuse or exploitation are prioritized for immediate review.

Cooperation with authorities

MixVy complies with applicable child safety laws. Where required by law, suspected CSAM or CSAE is reported to the relevant national authority (for example, the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children's CyberTipline in the United States) and we cooperate with law enforcement investigations and lawful requests.

Designated point of contact

Questions about these standards, or reports that need escalation beyond in-app reporting, can be sent to larrybesant@gmail.com. This contact is able to speak to MixVy's CSAE prevention practices and compliance.
