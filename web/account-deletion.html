Delete Your MixVy Account

MixVy is operated by Southern Supply Services.

Delete in the app

  1. Sign in to MixVy.
  2. Open Profile, then Account Center.
  3. Select Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE and confirm.
  5. If prompted, sign in again and repeat the request.

Request deletion without app access

Email larrybesant@gmail.com from the address associated with your account. Include your MixVy username if available. We may request additional information to verify account ownership.

What is deleted or retained

Account credentials, user-facing profile data, preferences, notification tokens, uploaded profile media, posts, blocks, friendships, and notifications associated with the account are deleted. Backup processing may take additional time. Operational logs may be retained for up to 30 days, and financial or safety records may be retained when required for fraud prevention, tax, legal, or active investigation purposes.
