MixVy Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

Who operates MixVy

MixVy is operated by Southern Supply Services. Questions and privacy requests can be sent to larrybesant@gmail.com.

Data we collect

We process account and profile details, sign-in identifiers, user-generated content, messages, room and call activity, safety reports, device and diagnostic data, notification tokens, approximate location when provided, and transaction records. Live audio or video is transmitted to provide room and call features. Recordings are collected only when a feature clearly says that recording is active.

How we use data

We use data to provide and secure MixVy, personalize social features, deliver notifications, process transactions, prevent fraud and abuse, investigate reports, maintain service reliability, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

Service providers and disclosure

We share only the data needed with providers that support authentication, hosting, databases, analytics, crash reporting, notifications, live communications, and payments, including Google Firebase, Agora, and Stripe when those features are enabled. We may disclose data when required by law or necessary to protect users, the public, or the service.

Retention and deletion

We retain account data while an account is active. Account deletion removes account and user-facing profile data, subject to backup processing and records we must preserve. Operational logs associated with a deleted account are retained for up to 30 days unless an active safety or legal investigation requires longer. Financial records required for tax, fraud prevention, or legal compliance may be retained for up to seven years.

Your choices and rights

You can manage profile visibility and preferences in the app and delete your account from Account Center. You may ask to access, correct, or export your data, or object to certain processing, by contacting larrybesant@gmail.com. We may verify your identity before completing a request, and local law may limit some requests.

Age, security, and changes

MixVy is for adults age 18 or older. We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Material policy updates will be identified by a new effective date and, when appropriate, an in-app notice.
